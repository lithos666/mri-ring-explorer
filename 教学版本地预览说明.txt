New configuration: 14 rings, 28 columns per ring, 1,736 cubes of 12×12×12 mm.
26 mm 表示相邻层磁铁端面净距，不是支撑或定位件厚度。
26 mm is the clear magnet-face gap, not support or locator thickness.

仍需核对 / Items requiring confirmation:
1. R05/R10 最外槽与 MATLAB 名义磁铁位置有约 0.2 mm 的内侧干涉。
   R05/R10 outer pockets have about 0.2 mm nominal inner-edge interference.
2. CAD 短螺丝标记 2.9×16 BF，材料图片为 M3×18；不能直接视作同一规格。
   CAD short screws specify 2.9×16 BF; the material reference specifies M3×18.
3. 亚克力实际分件 CAD、厚度和滑入路径的实物配合需要确认。
   Confirm acrylic CAD, thickness and the physical fit of its insertion path.
4. 打印材料、公差、打印配置、贯穿螺杆和框架切割清单、紧固工艺需补齐。
   Confirm printing materials/tolerances/settings, rods, frame cut lists and fastening procedures.
